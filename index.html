Zuntao Liu

Hi, I am a third-year master student at Northeastern University, advised by Prof. Zheng Fang and in close collaboration with Prof. Chen Wang. Before that, I completed my bachelor degree at Northeastern University.

My current research focuses on Robot Learning, Spatial Intelligence, Generative Models, and Multimodal LLMs. My long-term research goal is to develop scalable and robust generalist robotic systems that can understand, reason about, and interact with the 3D physical world, ultimately advancing embodied AI in complex and dynamic environments.
