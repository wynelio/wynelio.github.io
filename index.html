Hi, I am a third-year master student at Northeastern University, advised by Prof. Zheng Fang and in close collaboration with Prof. Chen Wang. Befor that, I completed my bachelor degree at Northeastern University.

My current research focuses on Multimodal LLMs, Generative Models, Event-based Vision, and Robotics. My long-term research goal is to develop intelligent systems with spatial understanding and reasoning of the 3D physical world, ultimately advancing embodied AI in dynamic and interactive environments.

I will graduate in June 2026 and am actively seeking research intern, RA, and PhD opportunities.
